Links ran one way. A guide declares the tools it hands off to, so guides
pointed at tools and nothing pointed back — leaving six new pages reachable
only from the footer while the 48 tool pages, which carry nearly all of this
site's internal links, pointed at none of them.

Nothing failed, because the inbound-link floor in check-seo only applied to
tools. An auditor that enforces a rule for one page type and not the one added
last week is how this goes unnoticed, so the check is extended rather than the
oversight simply fixed.

The tool page derives its guide links by inverting `Guide.tools` instead of
taking a `guides: string[]` field on `Tool`. The declared version would state
one relationship twice and let the halves drift: a guide pointing at a tool
that does not point back is invisible from the side holding the inbound links,
and nothing would complain. Inverting the single declaration means a guide
becomes reachable from its tools the moment it names them, and stops being
reachable the moment it stops.

The section sits above the ad and above the related tools. A reader who has
finished the FAQ and is still on the page has a question the widget did not
answer, which is what these pages are for.

Verified in the built export rather than in the source: unlock-file carries all
three zip guides, jwt-decoder carries the JWT one, merge-pdf carries both
privacy guides.
